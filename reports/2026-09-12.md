> **Note on timing:** Today, Saturday, September 12, 2026, is a weekend session. The dominant story is Friday's US August CPI release (8:30am ET / ~19:30 Bangkok), which landed **after** Thailand's ~17:30 Bangkok close: headline CPI rose 0.4% m/m (3.4% y/y) and core CPI rose 0.3% m/m — a tenth above the 0.2% forecast — pushing September Fed hike odds to roughly 85–90%. Global and Thai FX/gold markets are non-trading through the weekend, so this post-CPI bounce is the reference level carrying into today's single Saturday GTA quote.
