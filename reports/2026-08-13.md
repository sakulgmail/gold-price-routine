## Key Reasons
- The overnight global signal is decisively bullish: spot XAU/USD has extended to ~$4,410–4,428 after spiking to a fresh two-month-plus high of $4,438.30 on Wednesday's cooler-than-feared US CPI print — well above the ~$4,387–4,400 levels that prevailed during Wednesday's Thai session, leaving a real, still-largely-unpriced gap for today.
- Thailand's GTA rose only 350 baht Wednesday because the CPI print (and the bulk of the resulting rally) landed after Thailand's ~17:30 Bangkok close — a repeat of the recurring pattern where Thai gold lags the full size of a global move that happens during the US afternoon session.
- USD/THB is essentially flat (33.08, −0.02%), too small a move to offset the global gap.
- All three tracked sources were correct on their last call and remain aligned bullish: the Gold Research Center's survey (62–70% expecting gains), YLG/InterGold's intact uptrend framework, and FXStreet's now-validated CPI-conditional bullish targets ($4,430–4,492, then $4,500–4,600).
