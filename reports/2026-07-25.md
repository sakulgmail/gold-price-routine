# Thailand Gold Price Outlook — 2026-07-25

> **Note on timing:** Today, July 25, 2026, is a Saturday — and, for the first time, the Gold Traders Association of Thailand (GTA) is issuing **live weekend price updates**, not a single flat carry-forward quote as in past Saturdays (e.g. July 4, July 11, July 18). The GTA board resolved to start Saturday–Sunday announcements from today because CME Group has extended trading hours for certain gold futures contracts to cover the weekend, so Thai reference prices will now track ongoing global moves rather than freezing at Friday's close. Announcement timing and frequency are explicitly *not* fixed and will vary with international price moves, USD/THB, liquidity, volatility, and major events. No GTA quote for today had posted yet at research time (~07:00 Bangkok). Separately, the interbank USD/THB market itself remains closed over the weekend as usual, so the baht leg of the price will stay pinned at Friday's close even if global gold moves — a genuinely new asymmetry this Saturday call has to account for.

**Direction: ➡ SIDEWAYS** | Confidence: Low

Global spot XAU/USD closed Friday essentially flat, near **$4,052–4,053** (+0.06–0.08% on the session per TradingEconomics/Kitco), consolidating inside a well-defined **$4,021–4,085** range after Thursday's sharp ~2% plunge, and no fresh weekend move had been recorded at research time. No high-impact data lands today; the dominant catalyst is next Tuesday–Wednesday's FOMC decision (Sept-hike odds having risen toward ~80%), plus ongoing Middle East/Strait of Hormuz tensions keeping oil elevated — both two-way risks that sit outside today's window. Given a flat overnight setup, a source consensus that itself calls for consolidation/range-trade, and a modest but currently inactive baht headwind, the call leans SIDEWAYS. Confidence is Low rather than High (unlike prior "market closed" Saturdays) because this is the very first live-quoted weekend session under the new GTA policy — thin weekend liquidity and an unprecedented pricing mechanism mean today's actual move is genuinely harder to forecast than a normal weekday.
