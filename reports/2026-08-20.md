## Today's Prediction
**Direction: ▲ UP** | Confidence: High

Global spot XAU/USD has surged since yesterday's Thai close, from roughly $4,370–4,390 (the level being tested around Thailand's ~17:30 Bangkok close Wednesday) to about $4,515–4,520 now (Kitco, TradingEconomics) — a genuine ~3.0–3.4% overnight gain, the largest gap of this reporting window, and a fresh two-month high (above $4,550 intraday per Forbes). The catalyst has already fired and is behind us rather than ahead: the Treasury's Wednesday announcement that it would double its liquidity-support buyback operations for 10–30 year securities, combined with a softening dollar (DXY −0.7%) and falling yields, gave gold a decisive push through the July FOMC minutes release (three dissenters favored an immediate cut; the September hike-cut narrative has shifted firmly dovish since a weak July payrolls print). Crucially, Thailand's official Wednesday close (68,300, −450 baht) happened *before* this rally accelerated, meaning virtually none of the move is priced into the domestic quote yet — a large lagged catch-up move higher looks likely at today's open. USD/THB has strengthened to ~32.86 (from ~33.10–33.12 yesterday, about −0.7% to −0.8%), a real but comparatively small offset next to a >3% global surge. No high-impact US event lands specifically today — flash S&P PMIs are second-tier, and Jackson Hole doesn't open until tomorrow — which removes the volatility risk that has capped confidence at Medium on recent days, supporting a High-confidence call today even though the ultimate size of the domestic catch-up (a partial vs. full repricing) is the main source of remaining uncertainty.
