# Thailand Gold Price Outlook — 2026-09-17

> **Note on timing:** Today, Thursday, September 17, 2026, is a normal Thai trading day, the morning after the Fed's September FOMC decision. The Fed's statement landed at 2:00pm ET Wednesday (~1:00am Bangkok time Thursday) — after Wednesday's Thai gold session had already closed — so today is the first Thai session to fully digest it. Research was conducted early Bangkok morning, before GTA's first daily announcement (~09:00) and before YLG had published a fresh dated note for today. Wednesday, September 16 saw Thailand's GTA gold-bar price jump 900 baht (24 intraday adjustments) to 68,300 บาทละ, while global spot XAU/USD rallied to a Thai-close-time reference near $4,326–4,328 on softer oil prices and calmer Treasury yields ahead of the Fed. Overnight, the FOMC delivered a unanimous, hawkish 25-basis-point hike (to 3.75%–4.00%) — its first hike since 2023 — with Chair Kevin Warsh flagging further increases and the dot plot moving to 4.1% by year-end. Gold briefly touched $4,360 before the decision, then reversed sharply, sliding to around $4,265 in early Thursday Asian trade.

## Today's Prediction
**Direction: ▼ DOWN** | Confidence: High

Global spot XAU/USD has fallen roughly -1.4% overnight — from a Thai-close-time reference near $4,326–4,328 down to about $4,265 now — after the Fed's hawkish 25bp hike and Chair Warsh's hawkish guidance sent the dollar higher and non-yielding bullion sharply lower. USD/THB has weakened only modestly to 33.147 (+0.29% vs yesterday's 33.050 close), a partial but small mechanical cushion that comes nowhere close to offsetting the size of the global decline. FXStreet — the most accurate tracked source over the past three gradable sessions (3-for-3) — now frames gold as extending its slide toward the $4,250 support zone. The Gold Research Center's weekly survey (window Sept 14–18) continues to show a bearish retail lean (51% expecting declines vs. 37% rising), reinforcing the call. Unlike the past several days, today's main event risk (the FOMC decision) has already resolved rather than being still pending, and no other high-impact release overlaps today's Thai trading hours (US jobless claims, Philly Fed, and industrial production all land this afternoon US time, after Thai close) — the clarity and size of the overnight move support raising confidence to High.
