# Thailand Gold Price Outlook — 2026-07-15

> **Note on timing:** This report was compiled early in the Bangkok Wednesday morning, before the GTA's first intraday price announcement. The call below is a forward call built on the overnight global setup (largely the post-CPI rally that landed after Tuesday's Thai close), not same-day Thai price action.

## Today's Prediction
**Direction: ▲ UP** | Confidence: Medium

Global spot XAU/USD has jumped since yesterday's Thai close, last quoted near **$4,053** (+1.3% vs the ~$4,000 level that fed into yesterday's Thai close), after spiking as high as **$4,089–$4,102** (+~2.2%) immediately following a much-softer-than-expected June CPI print (headline −0.4% m/m, the largest monthly drop since April 2020; annual rate down to 3.5%). The report landed roughly two hours after Thailand's GTA closed for the day, so none of this move is in yesterday's Thai price — it is a fresh overnight gap for today. Treasury yields and the dollar both fell on the data, and traders slashed the odds of a near-term Fed hike (July hike probability down to ~15.5%). USD/THB is little changed to marginally softer for the baht (~33.5 vs ~33.3–33.4 earlier in the week), a roughly neutral-to-mildly-reinforcing input rather than an offset. The main risk to the call is timing: today's PPI print and Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's Senate Banking Committee testimony (both landing after Thailand's ~17:30 Bangkok close, mirroring yesterday's CPI pattern) could extend or reverse the move once Thai trading has already finished for the day, and the rally has already faded partway from its post-CPI spike — both reasons to cap confidence at Medium rather than High. Absent a reversal before the Thai open, the base case for today is a gap-up UP session tracking the overnight global rally.
