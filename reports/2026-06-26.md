# Thailand Gold Price Outlook — 2026-06-26

## Summary
**Direction: ▲ UP** | Confidence: Medium

Thailand gold broke its five-day losing streak on June 26, opening sharply higher by 550–600 baht (ทองแท่ง 63,600 บาทละ; ทองรูปพรรณ 64,450 บาทละ intraday) — a rebound from the seven-month lows hit on June 24–25. Globally, XAU/USD recovered above $4,000, hitting a session high near $4,080, after two key data releases softened near-term Federal Reserve rate-hike expectations: the May PCE deflator came in at 0.4% MoM (below the 0.5% forecast), and the University of Michigan's final June Consumer Sentiment showed one-year inflation expectations falling sharply to 4.6% from 5.0%. CME FedWatch September rate-hike probability dropped from 70% to 61% on the data. YLG Bullion frames $4,000 as a zone with "limited downside," pointing to 45% of central banks planning to increase gold holdings as a structural floor. This is a data-driven relief bounce, not a trend reversal; medium-term headwinds — sticky core PCE at 3.4% YoY, DXY at 2026 highs, and the 200-day SMA sitting $470 above at $4,474 — remain intact.

## Key Reasons

- **PCE softer than expected (primary catalyst):** May PCE headline 0.4% MoM (vs 0.5% forecast); annual 4.1% YoY, core 3.4% YoY. While still well above the Fed's 2% target, the month-on-month miss reduced immediate rate-hike urgency and triggered a relief rally in gold and risk assets.
- **UoM inflation expectations eased sharply:** June final Consumer Sentiment 49.5; year-ahead inflation expectations fell from 5.0% to 4.6%; long-run fell from 3.9% to 3.3% — the largest monthly drop in 2026. As a key Fed input metric, its decline reduces the probability of near-term rate action.
- **September rate-hike probability declined:** CME FedWatch moved from 70% to 61% probability for a September FOMC rate hike following the data, reducing the dollar tailwind that has been the primary headwind for gold throughout June.
- **$4,000 floor held as technical support:** XAU/USD tested but held $4,000 during June 24–25. Today's bounce above this level triggered short-covering and momentum buying. Immediate resistance: $4,070; targets $4,140 then $4,360 if sustained; failure below $4,000 would reopen $3,900.
- **Central bank structural demand floor:** YLG cited World Gold Council data showing 45% of central banks plan to increase gold reserves and 74% plan to reduce USD holdings — limiting structural downside even amid near-term pressure.
