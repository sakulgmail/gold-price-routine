# Thailand Gold Price Outlook — 2026-07-29

## Today's Prediction
**Direction: ▼ DOWN** | Confidence: Medium

Global spot XAU/USD has extended its slide overnight, from roughly **$4,042** at Tuesday's close to about **$4,016** now (down ~0.6–0.8%), continuing Tuesday's sharp ~1% drop as a firmer US dollar and rising Fed-hike odds keep pressuring bullion into FOMC decision day. Confidence is capped at Medium, not Low, because although the Fed's decision itself lands after today's Thai close, elevated pre-decision positioning risk and the chance of "sell the rumor" reversals or leaked/rumored dovish signals could interrupt the slide intraday.
