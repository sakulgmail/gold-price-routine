## Key Reasons

- **USD/DXY at 2026 high (primary driver):** Dollar Index above 101, its highest reading this year, making gold expensive for foreign buyers and pulling capital out of gold-denominated assets.
- **Fed rate-hike risk accelerating:** CME FedWatch showing ~50% probability for a September FOMC hike; October hike widely expected. Non-yielding gold loses appeal as Treasury yields remain elevated.
- **$4,000 psychological floor broken:** Gold closed below $4,000 on June 24 for the first time since November 2025. The technical break triggered stop-loss cascades and momentum short-selling.
- **AI-sector portfolio selling:** Investors sold gold to cover losses from AI-equity drawdowns, adding cross-market selling pressure on June 24–25 (reported by Thai PBS and Komchadluek).
- **Institutional downgrade:** Goldman Sachs cut its 2026 year-end gold target from $5,400 to $4,900 on June 19, reducing institutional buy demand.
- **Gold ETF outflows:** World Gold Council reports net −16 tonnes in May 2026, with outflows continuing into June — structural demand destruction, not just speculative unwinding.
