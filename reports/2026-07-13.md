## Today's Prediction
**Direction: ▼ DOWN** | Confidence: Medium

Global spot XAU/USD has fallen sharply since Friday's close (~$4,121.05), last trading near **$4,075** (−1.11%, −$45.87), after touching lows closer to $4,030–$4,050 intraday, as the US-Iran conflict escalated dramatically over the weekend: US CENTCOM carried out a third round of strikes on Iran (roughly 140 targets, 300+ over three nights) after an IRGC attack disabled a commercial vessel in the Strait of Hormuz, and Iran's Revolutionary Guards retaliated by striking sites across Qatar, Kuwait, Jordan and Oman. Despite the scale of this escalation, gold is trading **lower**, not higher — consistent with the pattern seen repeatedly over the past two weeks, where a Hormuz/Iran shock pushes oil and inflation expectations up, keeps the Fed's rate path hawkish, and lifts real yields and the dollar, a combination that has outweighed safe-haven demand for gold on every recent escalation day. USD/THB has firmed modestly to ~33.38–33.42 (from ~33.30–33.33 Friday), a mild but insufficient offset. No CPI or FOMC event falls within today's Thai trading hours (June CPI lands Tuesday July 14, just before Fed Chair Warsh's July 14–15 congressional testimony), but the fast-moving, now much larger regional conflict is a genuine two-way wildcard — a ceasefire or de-escalation headline could spark a sharp reversal just as easily as further strikes could deepen the move. Given the already-realized ~1.1% overnight decline but with unusually high event risk, the call for today is DOWN with Medium (not High) confidence.
