## Today's Prediction
**Direction: ▼ DOWN** | Confidence: High

Spot gold was near $4,140–4,150 around yesterday's Thai close. It then fell ~2.1% to ~$4,053 (intraday low ~$4,033) after US October CPI beat expectations (headline +0.4% m/m vs 0.3%, core +0.3% vs 0.2%). That pushed the 10-year Treasury yield above 5% and markets fully priced a 25bp Fed hike in December. The Thai price last traded near 65,100 sell, and a 2% global drop works out to roughly −1,000 to −1,400 baht. A weaker baht (likely, since the dollar strengthened) may soften the drop a little but should not reverse it. Expect a gap-down open and a weak-to-flat session.
