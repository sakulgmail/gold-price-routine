# Thailand Gold Price Outlook — 2026-08-04

**Direction: ▲ UP** | Confidence: Medium

With the overnight global move as the clearest signal and USD/THB neutral, the call leans UP, but confidence is capped at Medium given unresolved, conflicting US–Iran headlines (Trump says talks started; Iran denies) and this routine's mixed recent accuracy.
