**Direction: ▲ UP** | Confidence: Low

Global spot XAU/USD is consolidating near **$4,125–4,130** after touching a fresh two-week intraday high around **$4,167** overnight — a mild pullback, but still net positive versus the level around Thailand's July 22 close, extending (in decelerating form) the monster two-day rally that took Thai gold from 64,000 to 65,900 (+2.97% over two sessions). US initial jobless claims (week ending July 18) print later today (8:30am ET / ~7:30pm Bangkok), landing after the Thai session closes — a genuine wildcard that could reset the overnight tone for tomorrow but is unlikely to move today's Thai fix directly. Given the still-positive (if decelerating) overnight carry, the incremental baht tailwind, and the best-performing source staying constructive, the call leans UP — but confidence is capped Low given gold is sitting directly inside a flagged resistance band after two huge up-days, YLG (2-for-3) is explicitly cautioning against chasing, and today's jobless-claims print adds event risk to the broader picture.
