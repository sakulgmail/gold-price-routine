## Key Reasons

- **Hawkish Fed (primary driver):** New Fed Chair Kevin Warsh left rates unchanged (3.5%–3.75%) but revised the SEP to signal a 25 bps rate hike possible before year-end — a significant hawkish surprise that lifted the USD and bond yields, both headwinds for non-yielding gold.
- **Third consecutive weekly loss:** XAU/USD slid from ~$4,350+ intra-June highs to ~$4,155, a drop of roughly 4.5% over three weeks.
- **Bearish technical pattern:** 4-hour chart shows Bearish Engulfing and Three Black Crows candlestick formations with declining MACD momentum. Next key supports at $4,059.90 → $4,005.79 → $3,893.96.
- **Institutional downgrade:** Goldman Sachs cut its 2026 year-end gold forecast from $5,400 to $4,900, citing lower ETF inflows and delayed Fed rate cuts.
- **Heavy data week ahead:** June 23 — Flash PMIs; June 25 — GDP (Q1), Core PCE (May), Jobless Claims; June 26 — UMich inflation. Stronger-than-expected numbers would reinforce the hawkish narrative and add downside pressure.
- **Wall Street sentiment:** 70% bearish, 20% sideways, only 10% bullish for the coming week (Kitco, June 18).
