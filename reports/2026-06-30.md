- **Opened -1,000 baht with no intraday bounce:** Thailand gold opened at 62,650 (sell) and by mid-morning (9 adjustments by 11:04 AM) prices had returned to opening levels — sellers absorbed every attempted bounce, signalling no relief rally is underway today.

- **Hawkish Fed narrative intact:** September FOMC rate-hike probability ~70%; DXY near its 2026 high; elevated real Treasury yields suppress the appeal of non-yielding gold. Kitco summarised the dominant market narrative as "Fed repricing overwhelms haven demand."
- **Record quarterly decline:** Gold is set to finish Q2 2026 down ~14% — the steepest quarterly loss on record — with institutional stop-losses and ETF outflows (World Gold Council: −16 tonnes in May) continuing into June.
- **Technical picture deeply bearish:** Current price ~10% below the 200-day SMA ($4,413), 8% below 100-day SMA ($4,268), and 3% below 20-day SMA ($4,125). Death cross pattern noted by multiple analysts. Key downside targets cited: $3,900 (TD Securities near-term), $3,816 (October 2025 support), with a long-term technical target of $3,400 flagged by FinanceMagnates.
- **NFP on July 2 is the key tail-risk (not today):** A weak June payrolls print could send gold through $4,130–$4,200. But that catalyst is two days away; today's data calendar is light, leaving the macro and technical headwinds in full control.
