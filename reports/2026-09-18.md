No high-impact data lands during today's Thai trading hours (routine US data — jobless claims, Chicago Fed, leading indicators — lands this afternoon US time, after Thai close; no Fed speakers scheduled), and the FOMC's own outcome has already been digested, so the call is UP — but confidence stays at Medium rather than High given Thursday's large intraday round trip (a reminder that today's gains could stall or reverse near resistance) and the lone dissenting source.
