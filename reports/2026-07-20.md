## Today's Prediction
**Direction: ▼ DOWN** | Confidence: Medium

Global spot XAU/USD has gapped down over the weekend, sliding from the ~$4,018 level referenced by Thailand's Saturday fix to roughly **$3,989–3,991** now (~−0.7%, confirmed independently via Kitco and TradingEconomics), breaking back below the psychologically important $4,000 level. The move is not a classic Iran-war safe-haven rally — instead, markets are treating the escalating US-Iran conflict as an *inflation* event: oil has jumped on Strait of Hormuz risk, and CME FedWatch now prices a 73% chance of a December Fed hike (up from 50% a week earlier), with Dallas Fed's Lorie Logan and Vice Chair Philip Jefferson both floating openness to hikes. Higher real yields and a firmer dollar are outweighing the geopolitical risk premium. Compounding this, USD/THB has opened weaker (~33.48, vs. ~33.63 at Saturday's fix) — a stronger baht that further pressures the baht-denominated gold price on top of the global decline. Kitco's own July 17 framing explicitly flagged $4,000 as the line in the sand ("a break below $4,000 risks a deep short-term correction"), and that break has now occurred. Confidence is Medium rather than High because the Iran war remains a genuine two-way wildcard — a ceasefire headline or a fresh escalation could swing sentiment sharply intraday — and today's price discovery in Thailand has not yet begun.
