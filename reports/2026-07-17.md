> **Note on timing:** This report was compiled early in the Bangkok Friday morning, before the GTA's first intraday price announcement. The call below is a forward call built on the overnight global setup (an unresolved gap from Thursday's US-afternoon sell-off), not same-day Thai price action.

## Today's Prediction
**Direction: ▼ DOWN** | Confidence: Medium

Global spot XAU/USD sold off sharply on Thursday (July 16), sliding from Wednesday's ~$4,060 close to a session low near **$3,973** (settling around $3,976.58, roughly −2.1%), as resilient US data — retail sales +0.2% m/m, initial jobless claims down to a 10-week-low 208k — overwhelmed support from this week's softer CPI/PPI and pushed Treasury yields (10-year ~4.57%) and the dollar (DXY ~100.54) higher. Crucially, that decline was concentrated in Thursday's US afternoon session, **after** Thailand's GTA had already closed for the day, so Thailand's July 16 print absorbed only a fraction of it (−100 baht, −0.16%). As of this writing, spot has stabilized/bounced modestly off the $3,973 low to around **$3,985** (+0.21% on the latest session), but that level remains well below where global gold stood ahead of Thursday's rout — leaving an unresolved gap that today's Thai session is likely to catch up toward. USD/THB is little changed (~33.29 vs ~33.25 previous close), a negligible input. No FOMC-scale event or major US data release lands within today's Thai trading hours (industrial production, housing starts and prelim Michigan sentiment are second-tier; the next Fed decision isn't until July 29), which lowers scheduled-event risk versus recent days — but the still-unresolved Israel/Iran–US conflict remains a genuine two-way geopolitical wildcard (even as Trump has floated Iran signaling openness to renewed talks), which is why confidence stays at Medium rather than High. Absent a fresh de-escalation headline, the base case for today is a gap-down DOWN session as Thai gold catches up with Thursday's global weakness.
