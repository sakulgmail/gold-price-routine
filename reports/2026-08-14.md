## Today's Prediction
**Direction: ▼ DOWN** | Confidence: Medium

Global spot XAU/USD reversed hard on Thursday: after opening near $4,468.80 and holding in the $4,440s–$4,460s through most of Thai trading hours, gold sold off through the US afternoon — despite a cooler-than-expected July PPI (4.7% vs 4.9% forecast) and softer jobless claims (209K vs 202K forecast), both nominally dovish for the Fed — as traders booked profits after seven straight up sessions and an overbought RSI (67.49). Spot closed around $4,371.50 (Kitco), down 0.85% on the day, well below the ~$4,440–4,460 levels that prevailed during Thursday's Thai session. That leaves a real, still-largely-unpriced downside gap of roughly 1.5–2% for today, since Thailand's GTA close (68,750, −300 baht) only captured the early Asian pullback, not the deeper US-afternoon slide. USD/THB is only marginally weaker for the dollar (33.12 vs ~33.08, +0.12%) — far too small to offset. Source consensus is mixed: the most-accurate tracked source (YLG/InterGold, 3-for-3 the past three days) is already cautious near resistance and flagged a pullback toward its $4,345 (68,600 baht) support zone, while the two sources that stayed bullish (GRC survey, FXStreet) were both wrong-footed by Thursday's reversal and their own reference levels (FXStreet's $4,387.53 support) have already been breached. Confidence is capped at Medium: it's a lower-liquidity Friday pre-weekend session, RSI is cooling from overbought (raising bounce risk), and the medium-term uptrend structure (price well above the 21/50-day SMAs) remains technically intact.
