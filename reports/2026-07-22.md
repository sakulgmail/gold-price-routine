## Today's Prediction
**Direction: ▲ UP** | Confidence: Medium

Global spot XAU/USD is extending Tuesday's sharp rally, now trading near **$4,090** versus the ~$4,081–4,082 level at yesterday's global close (~+0.2–0.3% overnight), holding above the $4,072–4,080 zone and pressing toward Kitco's next resistance at $4,087.30/$4,162.36. Tuesday's session itself was a decisive break higher — short covering and renewed Iran-war safe-haven demand overwhelmed firm yields, a firmer dollar, and rising oil, pushing spot up ~1.86% and Thailand's gold-bar price up a sharp +750 baht (+1.17%) to 64,750. Kitco (this window's most reliable source) explicitly flagged a break above $4,040.90 as the trigger for a move toward $4,180–4,200, and that break has now happened, with its own July 21 PM piece framing gold's "risk/reward as flipped in investors' favor" even as it "struggles below $4,100." Partially offsetting the global strength, USD/THB has firmed slightly to ~33.64 from ~33.71 at yesterday's close (baht ~0.2% stronger), a mild headwind for the baht-denominated price. The dominant driver remains the US-Iran war — an 11th consecutive night of US strikes and a fresh Trump warning of "heavy attacks" on further nuclear-linked sites — but a genuine counter-signal has emerged: Iranian officials met mediators in Pakistan on Tuesday to weigh a 10-day ceasefire offer, a real two-way risk that could deflate the safe-haven bid quickly if it gains traction. No major high-impact US data lands today (Wednesday); the next catalysts are Thursday's jobless claims, Friday's flash PMI, and the July 28–29 FOMC decision. Given the live overnight continuation, Kitco's confirmed bullish trigger, and only a mild baht offset, the call leans UP — but confidence is capped at Medium given the elevated level after a big one-day gain, resistance directly overhead near $4,087–4,100, and the real chance of a ceasefire headline reversing sentiment intraday.
