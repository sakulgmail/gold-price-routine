## Key Reasons

- **Hawkish Fed repricing (primary driver):** 9 of 19 FOMC members now project at least one rate hike in 2026; Bank of America and Deutsche Bank both upgraded forecasts to three hikes. Markets are pricing the first hike as early as September, lifting the USD and Treasury yields — both direct headwinds for non-yielding gold.
- **Monday's rally unwound:** The +700 baht/+$53 bounce on June 22 was driven by US-Iran peace-talk optimism. Trump's subsequent threat of "decisive action" against Iran over nuclear compliance reversed that safe-haven calculus, removing the one bullish catalyst that had pushed gold higher.
- **Technical breakdown below $4,150:** XAU/USD closed ~$4,129, breaking below the key $4,150 support. Next support sits at $4,059.90; a break would expose $4,005 and potentially $3,894. RSI is approaching oversold territory (~36) but momentum is still negative.
- **Flash PMI and upcoming macro risk:** S&P Global Flash PMI (released today, June 23) and final Q1 GDP + May Core PCE (Thursday June 26) could add further downside if they reinforce the hawkish narrative with strong economic readings.
- **Bearish institutional backdrop:** Goldman Sachs already cut its year-end target from $5,400 to $4,900 last week. Combined with BofA's three-hike call, institutional sentiment has shifted decisively bearish.
- **High volatility:** 25 price adjustments in Thailand today (versus a typical 5–10) signals extreme uncertainty and rapid global repricing — an environment that historically skews toward the established downtrend.
