No CPI or FOMC event falls within today's Thai trading hours (US jobless claims land after the 17:30 Bangkok close; CPI isn't until July 14), but the unresolved, fast-moving Iran/Hormuz conflict is a real wildcard that could flip sentiment toward a safe-haven bid with little notice. With the fresh overnight move smaller than the last three days, USD/THB providing more offset, and this being the fourth consecutive day relying on the same "escalation = bearish for gold" pattern (raising the odds of a pause or technical bounce, echoing YLG's own "buy the dip" framing), the call for today is DOWN but with Medium — not High — confidence.
